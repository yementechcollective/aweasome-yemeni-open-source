## Our Commitment

Yemeni Open Source is maintained by [Yemen Tech Collective](https://yementc.org) and follows its community values of
**professionalism, neutrality, inclusion, respect, and knowledge sharing**. Everyone is welcome to take part regardless
of governorate, background, gender, age, disability, education, employer, or level of experience.

## Expected Behaviour

- Treat maintainers and contributors with professionalism and respect.
- Review the submission, never the person submitting it.
- Give feedback that is specific, factual, and actionable.
- Accept that curation decisions are editorial and are explained in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#review-and-curation).
- Credit other people's work, and link to the original repository rather than a copy.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption of a discussion.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Politically partisan content in issues, pull requests, or directory entries.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Submitting entries that are deceptive, plagiarised, or presented as someone else's work.

## Scope

This applies to every space this project uses — issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, and the content of
directory entries themselves — and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a concern by emailing **[info@yementc.org](mailto:info@yementc.org)**, or by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/yementechcollective/aweasome-yemeni-open-source/security/advisories/new)
if the matter is sensitive. Reports are handled confidentially, and the reporter is never identified without consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove any contribution that breaches this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or
permanently ban anyone who does so. Decisions are made by the maintainers of this repository and, where the matter
extends beyond it, by Yemen Tech Collective.
