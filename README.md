## About

**Yemeni Open Source** is a community-maintained directory of open-source software created or substantially maintained by
Yemeni technologists. It brings together **244 projects** in one place: Laravel and PHP packages, Flutter and Dart libraries,
Python and AI projects, JavaScript and TypeScript tools, C# and Go utilities, Arabic and RTL support, payment-gateway
integrations for Yemen and the Arab world, developer tooling, and technical learning resources — grouped into
[15 categories](#browse-by-category) you can browse.

If you are looking for a Yemeni open-source library, an Arabic-language developer tool, a payment integration for a
Yemeni or Gulf bank, or a project to contribute to, this is the place to start.

The directory exists to:

- **Showcase** high-quality open-source projects created or substantially maintained by Yemenis.
- **Help developers discover** useful libraries, tools, educational resources, and opportunities to contribute.
- **Give maintainers visibility** while preserving their ownership and independence.
- **Encourage collaboration**, knowledge sharing, and sustainable open-source development.

The catalog is generated from [`data/projects.yml`](data/projects.yml) and validated on every pull request, so the counts,
the ordering, and the categories stay consistent as the directory grows.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Projects remain in their original repositories and under the ownership and governance of their respective maintainers.
> Listing a project here does not transfer ownership to Yemen Tech Collective and does not constitute a security, quality,
> or maintenance guarantee.

## About Yemen Tech Collective

**Yemen Tech Collective (YTC)** is an independent Yemeni professional community that brings together engineers, developers,
designers, researchers, and technology specialists. It aims to transfer knowledge and experience, strengthen the
capabilities of Yemeni technologists, and build connections between them and professionals and institutions around the world.

- Website: [yementc.org](https://yementc.org)
- GitHub: [github.com/yementechcollective](https://github.com/yementechcollective)
